# North star

**Do music full time**, which requires an O-1B visa and, later, a green card.

Getting signed is a means, not the goal.

## What counts as progress
1. **Visa evidence:** press, festival/showcase slots, headlining distinguished venues, expert letters, commercial milestones. O-1B needs strong evidence for at least 3 of 6 criteria plus a petitioner (can't self-petition).
2. **Draw:** tickets sold in NYC, size of the text/email list.
3. **Finished songs:** the asset everything else multiplies.

Views and followers count only when they move one of these.

## Objective: end of 2026 — "ready for when the ads stop"
- [ ] O-1B immigration lawyer gap analysis: which 3 criteria are closest
- [ ] Evidence folder started (press, posters, contracts, payments, stats screenshots)
- [ ] Band agreement signed with music lawyer
- [ ] New sound direction locked, 2 demos done with Gavin
- [ ] List at 150
- [ ] One shareable, non-song content format proven twice

## Objective: 2027 — "O-1B ready, sold-out headline, no ads"
- [ ] EP released
- [ ] Sold-out headline at Baby's All Right (or a room that size)
- [ ] List at 500 or more
- [ ] 3 or more criteria with strong evidence, petitioner lined up
- [ ] 3 or more serious label conversations (DIY labels first)

Not a key result: "get signed." It's a lagging outcome we don't control.
