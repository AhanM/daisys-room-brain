# Strategy (80/20)

Status: hypotheses. Confirm or kill after the time and spend audit.

## Likely 20% (do more)
- **Events as content.** The only viral hit (~145K views, ~3,300 sends) came from event-invite mechanics. Song reels cap around 9K views and ~80 sends.
- **Finishing songs.** Everything multiplies them.
- **A few high-leverage people.** Nautics, Rachel, Lexi, Sam: each one step from a label or booker.
- **Gatekeeper recognition.** Press, festivals, venues and expert letters count for the visa and for labels.

## Likely 80% (cut or shrink)
- Posting 3x a day of song reels. Try 1 a day, shift to shareable formats.
- More hook tweaking. The winning hook is already found.

## Virality
High-variance bet with a capped budget (about 30% of music time), not the whole strategy. Shows make the content that gets shared; that content sells the next show.

## Money
Judge every dollar by cost per north-star outcome (evidence, list signups, tickets, finished songs), not cost per stream.
- High: immigration lawyer consult, songs, EP press push, festival applications, band agreement
- Medium: US-only ads judged per signup or ticket, then tapered; cheap visualizers
- Low: global ads, full music videos, polish on content that doesn't spread
- Flag: the Schenke credit and relationship carry credibility value for labels and visa letters.
